# Shield Breaker

[![](https://img.youtube.com/vi/b9A9MYZ2oa0/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/b9A9MYZ2oa0)

Adds tunnel-digging TNT inspired by shield tunneling machines. It comes in three lengths: 30m, 50m, and 100m, and digs a straight 3×3 tunnel. Ores and wood are not destroyed, and no torches are placed, so you'll need to finish the tunnel by hand. Blocks destroyed by the tunnel do not drop items.

## How to Use

*   Get a Shield Breaker from the recipe or the Creative inventory.
*   Place it where you want to start digging, then ignite it with Flint and Steel or a Fire Charge. It digs in the direction opposite the face you clicked.
*   It can also be ignited with a redstone signal. It digs away from the side the signal comes from.
*   The tunnel is dug 4 seconds after ignition. It deals no damage.

## Other Information

*   Enable both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.
*   Digging the 100m tunnel takes a little time.
